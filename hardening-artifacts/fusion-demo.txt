HONEST FINDING: lambda has NO measurable effect at this corpus scale.
Multi-group-within-one-family cases are rare in RANGE-SIM v0.1, so the
damping term almost never binds. What actually moves the false-merge
rate is the grouping step itself - collapsing raw retrievals and
correlated captures onto one independence key (see ablation: 3.5% ->
1.5%). We report lambda as an unvalidated design prior, not a result.
